Featured Destinations

Hand-picked highlights from coast to coast

Canada stretches over 10 million square kilometres — the world's second-largest country. Whether you're chasing northern lights in the Yukon, tasting fresh lobster in Nova Scotia, or skiing perfect powder in the Rockies, there's a corner of Canada that will captivate you.

Alberta

Banff National Park

Canada's oldest national park delivers jaw-dropping scenery — electric-blue glacial lakes, wildlife at every turn, and world-class ski resorts.

British Columbia

Vancouver

Mountains meet the Pacific Ocean in one of the world's most liveable cities — explore Stanley Park, Granville Island, and the vibrant food scene.

🗓 Jun–Sep Explore
Ontario

Toronto

Canada's largest and most multicultural city — world-class dining, iconic neighbourhoods, museums, and the CN Tower's thrilling EdgeWalk.

🗓 May–Oct Explore
Ontario

Niagara Falls

One of the world's most famous natural wonders — 3,160 tonnes of water per second, boat tours into the mist, and dazzling night illuminations.

Québec City

North America's only walled city north of Mexico — cobbled streets, French culture, the grand Château Frontenac, and a magical Winter Carnival.

Why Visit Canada?

A few of the many reasons travellers fall in love with the True North

🏔️

Stunning Nature

From the Rockies to the Bay of Fundy, Canada's landscapes are unmatched in scale and raw beauty.

🌍

Multicultural Cities

Toronto, Vancouver, and Montréal rank among the world's most diverse and welcoming urban centres.

🦌

Wildlife Encounters

Spot grizzly bears, moose, beluga whales, and polar bears in their natural habitats.

🍁

Four Seasons

Fall foliage, winter skiing, spring wildflowers, or summer festivals — every season is spectacular.

🍽️

World-Class Food

From poutine and fresh Maritime lobster to Vancouver sushi and Montréal smoked meat — Canada's food scene thrills.

🛡️

Safe & Friendly

Canada consistently ranks among the world's safest, most welcoming countries for international travellers.
